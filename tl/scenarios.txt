# Scenario titles and intros (see tools/scenarios.py); N = SNR file 0-8 (PS2 scenario N+1)
0.title	Turmoil
0.intro	1554... The alliance of Imagawa, Hojo and Takeda sealed at Zentokuji in Suruga makes the eastern lords regroup. In the west, Sue Harukata, who drove out his lord, and Amago Haruhisa, who destroyed his kin, lose the people's hearts, and Mori Motonari grows strong. Oda Nobunaga of Owari, wed to Kicho, daughter of Saito Dosan, is now 21. This is the year a monkey-like youth appears before him...
1.title	The Sun
1.intro	1560... Imagawa Yoshimoto, lord of the Tokai who counts himself kin to the shoguns, marches on Kyoto to bring peace to a realm in turmoil. Barring his road stands the Fool of Owari, Oda Nobunaga. Meanwhile Nagao Kagetora of Echigo, sheltering the former Kanto deputy Uesugi Norimasa, eyes the Kanto. The Hojo and Takeda, with their own ambitions there, will not stand by...
2.title	Rising Dragon
2.intro	1568... From Gifu Castle, Oda Nobunaga presses his "Rule by Force", scatters his foes in the home provinces and enters Kyoto with Ashikaga Yoshiaki. The Miyoshi and the merchants of Sakai fear him. But the new shogun is no puppet. Stirred by Yoshiaki, Asakura, the Honganji faithful and Mori will soon rise against Nobunaga, and the key to the league is the giant of the east, Takeda Shingen...
3.title	Supremacy
3.intro	1575... Nobunaga has crushed Azai and Asakura and survived his worst crisis, yet his foes are far from beaten. Honganji rallies the faithful across the land with Mori behind it, and Oda lacks the navy to cut them apart. Takeda Katsuyori, entrenched in Kai and Shinano, challenges Tokugawa Ieyasu. Answering the call, Nobunaga marches to Nagashino with new tactics and a mass of guns...
4.title	Upheaval
4.intro	1582... The Takeda, their glory gone, face their end. Nobunaga's conquest nears completion: Shibata Katsuie attacks Uesugi Kagekatsu in Hokuriku, Hashiba Hideyoshi presses Mori Terumoto in Chugoku, and Takigawa and Tokugawa hold the east. Yet the flaw lies at the centre: Akechi Mitsuhide, who will rule the realm for 11 days and be remembered as its greatest traitor...
5.title	Great Peace
5.intro	1598... With the death of the Taiko, the Toyotomi peace crumbles. Tokugawa Ieyasu plays the officials led by Ishida Mitsunari against the generals who hate them to split the Toyotomi lords. Mori, Uesugi and Ukita resent him, and the realm divides into Toyotomi and Tokugawa. Their clash will come at Sekigahara, just five ri from Sunomata, where the late Taiko first made his name...
6.title	Exile
6.intro	1549... The missionary Francis Xavier lands at Kagoshima, preaches in Kyoto, Yamaguchi and Bungo, then leaves Japan. The western lords crave Nanban culture and buy the new matchlocks. This year Miyoshi Nagayoshi, with Hosokawa Ujitsuna as his puppet, drives the shogun from Kyoto and rules the home provinces. In Mikawa, Matsudaira Hirotada, father of Ieyasu, is murdered by a retainer...
7.title	Dreams
7.intro	Was it the gods, or demons? A thick fog covered all Japan, and when it lifted, a dream world appeared where none could tell truth from lies. Every warlord met at once: some became ronin, some rose to daimyo, all traded places and scattered, seeking dreams within the dream. Zhuangzi said: "Did I dream I was a butterfly, or does a butterfly dream it is me?" This floating world is but a dream...
8.title	Naniwa
8.intro	1584... Having beaten Akechi Mitsuhide and Shibata Katsuie after Honnoji, Hashiba Hideyoshi has Oda's old retainers under him and most of the realm in hand. Yet some cannot be ignored: Sanada Masayuki in the old Takeda lands, Chosokabe Motochika, who would unite Shikoku... Above all Tokugawa Ieyasu, Nobunaga's ally, now lord of five lands, who joins Nobunaga's son Nobukatsu against Hideyoshi...
